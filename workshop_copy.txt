## Description
(Full Workshop description)

[h1]Court of Shadows[/h1]
[b]Lasombra culture & faith for Princes of Darkness[/b]

An Umbral companion for [b]Princes of Darkness[/b]: courtly Lasombra culture, Via Tenebrarum, and rites of shadow, desire, and blood. Build a Court of Shadows, guide childer through sacrament, and rule the night as doctrine.

[h2]Requires[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Princes of Darkness[/b]
[*] [b]More Tenets Slots[/b]
[*] [b]More Tenets Slots – Princes of Darkness-Patch[/b]
[/list]

[h2]Load order[/h2]
[olist]
[*] Princes of Darkness
[*] More Tenets Slots
[*] More Tenets Slots – Princes of Darkness-Patch
[*] Court of Shadows
[/olist]

[i]Vamp-Stable GUI Patch is optional (portrait/GUI stability). Not required for this mod.[/i]

[h2]What you get[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Umbral culture & Via Tenebrarum[/b] — tenets, ethos, and a faith built for Lasombra courts (includes Communion-style indulgences support and Nox Nuda)
[*] [b]Umbral Crossing[/b] — Court sacrament of the Embrace (scheme + ceremony), separate from Question Embrace trials
[*] [b]Umbral Baptism[/b], Shadow Adoption, and household language study
[*] [b]Umbral Council & court positions[/b] — specialized seats and tasks for a shadowed realm
[*] [b]Salon of Night lifestyle[/b] and [b]Grant Lifestyle Access[/b] for unlanded prodigies under your rule (courtiers, vassals, spouses, concubines, children)
[*] Activities & court life — Black Chapel Procession, Veiled Pursuit, Umbral Orgy, consorts, Mirrorless Court, Black Ledger, and more
[*] Dynasty renown package for Court of Shadows play (HoF / dominion / conversion / holy sites support)
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*] Designed for [b]CK3 1.19[/b] with current PoD
[*] [b]Nox Nuda[/b] is the faith-layer for naked adherents; Crossing ceremony portraits stay clothed for stability
[*] If a Question Embrace trial gets stuck, use the [b]Abandon Embrace Trial[/b] decision
[*] Debug decisions are debug-mode only and not part of normal play
[/list]

[h2]Not required[/h2]
Other vampire GUI/portrait patches, Infinite Wards, or beauty mods. Add those only if you want them for their own features.

[h2]Feedback[/h2]
Report load order, PoD version, and what you were doing if something breaks. Thanks for playing in the dark.
