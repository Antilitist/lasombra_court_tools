## lasombra_court_procession_nave_open
# Event 1100 — The Black Chapel Opens (spotlight guest)
A vaulted Black Chapel nave at vigil's first hour, votaries gathering between black marble pillars while incense and ash drift through purple candle-smoke. The high altar gleams with blood-wine and spent tapers; masked courtiers in dark liturgical dress turn toward the host's empty judgment seat. Wide cinematic composition, warm ember light against cold abyssal shadow, painterly historical-fantasy illustration.

## lasombra_court_procession_vigil_tone
# Event 1100b — Tone of the Vigil
The same chapel nave held in suspended breath after the opening prayer — three moods implied in one frame: solemn sacrament at the altar, welcoming appetite along the side aisles, and dread pooling beneath the rafters. Candle-rows lead the eye to a shadowed pontifical chair; ash motes hang in the air like a decision not yet spoken. Intimate wide shot, rich chiaroscuro, gothic night-purple palette.

## lasombra_court_procession_confession_rail
# Event 1102 — Confession at the Rail
A side-altar confessional rail in near-darkness, wrought iron and velvet kneeler worn smooth by secret sins. A single votary kneels in profile while the host's silhouette looms beyond a guttering candle; the chapel behind them dissolves into violet murk. Tension between penance and provocation, close-middle wide framing, whispered intimacy, painterly CK3 event art.

## lasombra_court_procession_blood_wine
# Event 1103 — Blood-Wine Sacrament
Altar steps at the vigil's hunger: a chased silver cup of blood-wine passed between shadowed hands, ruby liquid catching candlelight. Faithful blur in the nave while the altar cloth, spent roses, and ash smears tell of a rite tipping from prayer into appetite. Luxurious sacrilegious warmth, shallow depth of field on the cup, decadent gothic color grade.

## lasombra_court_procession_pulse_confession
# Pulse: Lustful Confession (hear_confession)
A votary leans to the confessional grille, lips parted on a lustful whisper; the lattice throws barred shadow across their face. The host listens from the dark side of the rail, only a jeweled hand and candle reflected in brass. Claustrophobic side-chapel mood, secretive, dangerous honesty.

## lasombra_court_procession_pulse_indulgence
# Pulse: Indulgence Granted (grant_indulgence)
A pontifical hand extends a wax-sealed indulgence scroll above a bowed votary while the Long Night's revel is hinted in the blurred background — loosened collars, laughter without faces. Mercy as seduction; gold seal and purple ribbon against soot-dark stone, soft forgiving light.

## lasombra_court_procession_pulse_sanctify
# Pulse: Chapel Sanctified (sanctify_chapel)
The chapel font and altar being anointed with chrism and shadow-smoke, Via Tenebrarum sigils chalked on black marble. Acolytes in hooded black vestments ring the nave; consecration feels hungry rather than chaste. Ascending perspective toward the altar, holy smoke and violet gleam.

## lasombra_court_procession_pulse_ash
# Pulse: Ash Liturgy (ash_liturgy)
Close ritual on ash: a silver thurible and dish of pale ash, fingers marking a votary's tongue or brow in the Long Night rite. Rows of bowed silhouettes, candles doubled in polished floor stones. Stark, tactile, penitential — gray ash on warm skin tones, smoky atmosphere.

## lasombra_court_procession_pulse_blood
# Pulse: Blood Absolution (blood_absolution)
The high altar awash in steam from a blood absolution basin, crimson droplets on white linen and a raised ceremonial knife laid beside prayer books. Faithful shadows kneel in concentric ranks; the scene feels like mercy sharpened into blade-edge solemnity. Dramatic uplighting, crimson and indigo contrast.

## lasombra_court_procession_pulse_seductive
# Pulse: Seductive Absolution (seductive_absolution)
Absolution at the rail becoming flesh: two figures nearly touching across the confessional divide, breath and candle-flame mingling, a fallen veil or glove on the stone floor. Sacrament and seduction inseparable; warm rose-gold highlights on skin, chapel background dissolved to bokeh shadow.

## lasombra_court_procession_pulse_carnal
# Pulse: Carnal Sacrament (carnal_sacrament)
A side chapel curtained with black silk for the carnal sacrament — disheveled liturgical linens, overturned chalice, two entwined silhouettes implied behind gauze rather than shown. Oblique tasteful framing through curtain slits; heat, secrecy, post-rite stillness, deep shadow.

## lasombra_court_procession_pulse_benediction
# Pulse: Shadow Pontiff (pontiff_benediction)
The Shadow Pontiff elevated on the nave steps, arms wide in benediction, congregation prostrate in a sea of candles. Abyssal shadow seems to pour from the rafters toward the faithful; majesty and menace united. Low-angle heroic composition, vast vertical space, purple-black grandeur.

## lasombra_court_procession_pulse_wine
# Pulse: Blood-Wine Communion (wine_communion)
Communion circle in the nave: chained silver ewer passed hand to hand among masked votaries, each mouth stained faintly red. Symmetrical composition down a candle aisle toward the altar; communal hunger, glossy painterly highlights on metal and lips.
